## No-Cost Workflow

1. Open `shorts_recorder.html` in Chrome or Edge.
2. Pick a Short from the dropdown.
3. Click `Record WebM`.
4. When recording finishes, download the `.webm`.
5. Upload as YouTube Short.
6. Use title/description from `shorts_metadata.csv`.
