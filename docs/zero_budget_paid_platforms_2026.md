# Zero-Budget Paid Platforms 2026

Date: 2026-06-11

This tracks non-agency paths that do not require buying inventory, ad spend, paid SaaS, or paid training. These paths still require account creation, identity checks, payout setup, and acceptance by the platform or customer.

## Priority Signup Queue

1. Respondent
   - Path: paid research studies, interviews, focus groups, surveys.
   - Official page: https://www.respondent.io/become-a-participant
   - Why it fits: Respondent says participants can get paid for research studies, surveys, and focus groups, with remote and in-person studies and virtual Visa payouts.
   - Best profile angle: AI tools, cybersecurity, GitHub, freelancer, Indonesia, small-business automation.
   - Blocker: human signup, profile, screening answers, payout eligibility.

2. Prolific
   - Path: paid online studies and AI evaluation tasks.
   - Official page: https://www.prolific.com/participants
   - Why it fits: Prolific says it pays real money, has voluntary tailored tasks, and cashes out through PayPal once minimum earnings are reached.
   - Best profile angle: AI evaluation, software/automation, security, Indonesian bilingual perspective.
   - Blocker: waitlist and identity verification.

3. UserTesting
   - Path: surveys, recorded usability tests, live conversations.
   - Official page: https://www.usertesting.com/get-paid-to-test
   - Why it fits: UserTesting lists surveys, recorded tests, live video conversations, PayPal payouts, and no contributor fee.
   - Best profile angle: technical user, AI tools, websites, SaaS onboarding, mobile/desktop testing.
   - Blocker: practice test, microphone, PayPal.

4. Rent A Cyber Friend
   - Path: paid one-to-one video conversation.
   - Official page: https://www.rentacyberfriend.com/
   - Why it fits: It describes a human connection marketplace where Cyber Friends share time and experience through one-to-one video calls and set their schedule.
   - Best profile angle: Indonesian/English conversation, work stress, AI tools, online safety, practical problem sorting.
   - Blocker: human account setup, profile approval, platform demand.

5. Premium.Chat
   - Path: paid text chat, video calls, consulting, support services.
   - Official page: https://premium.chat/
   - Why it fits: It supports text chat, video calls, flat-rate or per-minute fees, and payouts by PayPal or direct deposit.
   - Best profile angle: AI workflow help, online safety checks, calm paid conversation.
   - Blocker: signup, payout method, audience/customer traffic.

6. Fiverr
   - Path: seller marketplace gig.
   - Official page: https://www.fiverr.com/
   - Best gig angles:
     - "I will be your calm listener for work stress and practical next steps"
     - "I will help you understand AI tools and automation safely"
     - "I will review your GitHub or online profile for trust"
   - Blocker: gig approval, competition, buyer traffic.

7. AI training platforms
   - Path: AI response rating, coding evaluation, safety evaluation, data annotation.
   - Examples to check manually: Outlier, DataAnnotation, Clickworker, Toloka.
   - Best profile angle: AI, Python, GitHub, security, bilingual Indonesian/English.
   - Blocker: platform availability by country, tests, inconsistent work, ethical boundaries.

## Safety Rules

- Never pay to join an earning platform.
- Never buy or rent verified accounts.
- Never use VPN/geospoofing to bypass country rules.
- Never claim to be a therapist, doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor.
- Refuse crisis counseling and direct people to emergency/local professional help.
- Keep adult/romantic/sexual content out of the paid-listening profile.
- Use platform payments where possible.
