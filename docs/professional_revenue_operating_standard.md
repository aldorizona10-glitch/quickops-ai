# Professional Revenue Operating Standard

Last updated: 2026-06-11 WIB

This is the operating standard for QuickOps AI revenue work. The goal is real money, zero upfront spend, and professional execution.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not send duplicate cold outreach to the same email address.
- Do not send generic spam. Every message must be specific to the prospect's product, role, or public request.
- Do not claim revenue until payment is actually received.
- Do not claim a bug, security issue, or payout unless the evidence is real and reproducible.
- Do not run active security testing without written authorization or official bug bounty/VDP scope.
- Do not use weak portfolio links. Use only the main agency, profile, start, and security pages.
- Do not ask for payment before scope is confirmed, except when the prospect explicitly wants to proceed.

## Revenue Lanes

- Core agency workflow: lead intake, CRM/spreadsheet state, follow-up templates, and handover.
- AI security team review: red, blue, purple, yellow, green, and orange team coverage for one authorized workflow.
- Bug bounty: official scope only; report only with valid PoC and impact.
- Open-source bounty: only explicit payout, unsaturated issue, and clean implementation path.

## Outreach Quality Bar

Each outbound message must include:

- The exact reason this prospect was selected.
- One narrow USD 100 offer.
- A clear safety boundary.
- Relevant public links.
- No pressure language.
- No repeated email to a previously contacted recipient unless they replied or explicitly requested follow-up.

## Delivery Quality Bar

Each paid job must produce:

- Confirmed scope.
- Written permission for security work when applicable.
- A concise deliverable with findings, steps, recommendations, and next action.
- Handover notes that the client can use without depending on the operator.
- Payment status recorded separately from sent/reply status.

## Current Truth

- Sent count is not revenue.
- Replies are not revenue.
- Open PRs are not revenue.
- Only confirmed payment is revenue.
