# Delivery Kit - USD 100 Lead Workflow

This is the operational checklist for delivering the first paid workflow.

## Scope Boundary

One USD 100 workflow includes:

- One intake source, such as form, email inbox, WhatsApp link, Stripe email, or manual CSV.
- One destination, such as Google Sheets, HubSpot, Airtable, or Notion.
- Up to five fields.
- Up to five status stages.
- Three follow-up templates.
- One simple dashboard or status view.
- One handover doc and operator checklist.

Not included:

- Paid ads.
- Bulk cold email sending.
- Complex custom code.
- Unlimited revisions.
- Revenue guarantees.
- Multiple departments or multiple workflows.

## Intake Questions

Ask the client:

1. What is the first trigger?
2. Where does the information currently go?
3. What gets missed or delayed?
4. Which tools do you already use?
5. What fields must be captured?
6. Who follows up?
7. What should happen after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days?
8. What does "done" mean for this workflow?

## Default Pipeline

Use this when the client has no process:

- New
- Contacted
- Waiting
- Won
- Lost

Default fields:

- Name
- Email or phone
- Source
- Need/request
- Status
- Next follow-up date
- Notes

## Delivery Steps

1. Confirm scope in writing.
2. Confirm payment method.
3. Receive payment.
4. Create or clean the destination sheet/CRM.
5. Add fields and stages.
6. Add sample leads.
7. Write follow-up templates.
8. Add instructions.
9. Test with one sample inquiry.
10. Send handover message.

## Handover Message

Hi {{name}},

The workflow is ready.

Included:

- intake path
- CRM/sheet pipeline
- status stages
- follow-up templates
- simple dashboard/status view
- handover notes

Recommended daily use:

1. Check all `New` leads.
2. Move each lead to `Contacted`.
3. Set `Next follow-up date`.
4. Use the reminder template when a lead is waiting.
5. Move closed leads to `Won` or `Lost`.

Let me know if anything in the handover needs clarification.
